---
**webai.blog.br** collects texts about models that run in the browser: what was tested, how it was measured and what worked or didn't.

Anyone can propose a post. The blog is an open GitHub repository: you write a Markdown file, open a pull request and, once approved, the text goes on the site. The step-by-step is in the [contribution guide](https://github.com/eduardoworrel/webai.blog.br/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Posts can be published in Portuguese, English or both. To follow along, subscribe to the [RSS feed](feed.xml).
